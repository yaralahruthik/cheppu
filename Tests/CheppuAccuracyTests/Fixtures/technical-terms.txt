Parakeet TDT version three runs on CoreML through FluidAudio, and Cheppu resamples the captured audio to sixteen kilohertz mono before the decoder ever sees it. The encoder is quantised to int eight rather than int four, because the smaller download trades away the accuracy the engine was chosen for. Swift Package Manager builds the whole thing, and the headless core imports no operating system framework at all, so its suite runs with nothing granted and nothing plugged in. Accessibility is what lets the event tap watch the keyboard, and the clipboard fallback catches whatever the accessibility API refuses to insert.
