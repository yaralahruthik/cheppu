Hruthik maintains Cheppu on GitHub. It depends on FluidAudio from Fluid Inference, which packages the Parakeet model that NVIDIA published on Hugging Face. It builds with Xcode on macOS Sequoia, runs on Apple Silicon, and installs through Homebrew. Anthropic makes Claude, Apple makes the Neural Engine, and Spokenly and Superwhisper are the apps that showed how good on device dictation on a Mac can be.
